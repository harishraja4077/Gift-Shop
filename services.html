The method

Four steps, five working days

We will email you at the end of every one of them. No silence, no chasing.

01

Brief

A 30-minute call. Who it is for, what they love, what they already own, and the date it must arrive.

02

Composition

We lay the collection out on the studio table, photograph it, and send you the proposal with two alternatives.

03

Approval

You approve or amend. We then tie, seal, letterpress and photograph the finished box before it is packed.

04

Dispatch

Sealed, tracked and collected by a carbon-neutral courier. You get the tracking link and the studio photograph.
