Asked often enough

Six questions we answer several times a day. If yours is not here, it goes straight to the studio.

Send a Brief

Within one working day, and usually within a few hours if you write before noon. If your date is more than eight weeks out, expect a little longer because we will want to check maker availability first.

Three things help enormously: who it is for, the date it must arrive, and a budget range. Everything else — colour preferences, allergies, what they already own — is useful but we can ask. You do not need to know what you want.

No. Write to us, we propose a composition with photographs, you approve it, and we send a secure payment link. There is nothing to sign up for before we have talked.

We ship to forty-one countries, carbon-neutral, duties prepaid above £250. A handful of destinations cannot take perishable pantry items in warm months, and we will always say so before you pay rather than after.
